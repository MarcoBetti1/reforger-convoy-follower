From the repository root in PowerShell:

```powershell

```

The scene inherits Game Master Arland. Its `default.layer` places a US spawn point, three Convoy Driver groups, and four empty vanilla M923A1 transport trucks on the US base apron. One truck is near the player spawn; each driver is about 3.8 m from its intended truck and at least 13 m from the next closest one. The original driver, trucks, and spawn were previously seen in World Editor; the new placements passed Workbench validation and packaging but still need a visual/gameplay check. The World Editor launch helper's `--execute` and `-load` path worked for GM Arland and the saved subscene.

The separate [Everon night demo guide](../../docs/convoy-everon-demo.md) describes the Regina to Levie route, player LAV, staged supplies, and night scenario. Its geometry and night start still need a player check.

## Current convoy redesign and player test

The current source forms one convoy per player, with a hard cap of five trucks. **Unit One follows the player's vehicle; Unit Two follows Unit One's truck; each later unit follows the truck immediately ahead.** Only the convoy leader speaks to the ordering player. The session routes the leader's own events to short "we/us" calls and relays later units' range, stuck, lost, and rejoined events as numbered Unit N calls. Routine ready, following, and holding updates from later units are silent. The first chain build was observed following and its processed voice calls were audible. A later F5 run captured two optional generated-pack calls; the final manual control loop still needs a player test.

### Destination unloading and return

When the player's lead vehicle stops for several seconds, followers approach it in order and stay seated; exiting the vehicle also starts this arrival behavior. No cargo is loaded or unloaded by the addon. At the stopping spot, the owner can unload the front **M923A1 or standard Ural4320 transport** manually and choose one of two distinct release orders. **Pull off and regroup** is outside at that truck's existing rear cargo interaction menu; it sends the truck toward a rear return slot. **Pull ahead and wait** is available in that same rear menu or in the convoy map panel after selecting the front truck. The player should park at least about 8 m off the travel lane. The current preflight requires a connected, unoccupied forward road slot with at least 8 m nominal mapped road width; that is a minimum check, not a guarantee of physical passing space around trees or obstacles. Neither order is automatic. The next truck approaches the same unload spot only after the released truck has physically parked and cleared it. A failed maneuver should hold the line rather than advance it. A wide-road autonomous run parked two trucks ahead and advanced the third into the unload bay; its owner-vehicle bypass was blocked, so the full pass and rejoin remain unverified. The new forward rear-menu entry itself has not yet been clicked live. The rear return route failed its most recent strict live gate on a tight turnaround.

Rear-released trucks form a parked return line and do not follow the player just because the player enters a vehicle. The intended return event occurs when the owner drives the lead vehicle homeward past that line, or uses **Regroup convoy for return** at a parked truck's rear menu. Forward-released trucks form a separate line ahead; they do not join on a homeward crossing. To bring them along, the owner must drive past the farthest parked-ahead truck and explicitly choose **Resume ahead line after passing** in the panel. The forward resume and rear return merge still need live verification. There is no autonomous route to a stored base.

The prototype convoy panel opens over the normal map while the owner has an active convoy. It displays the server roster and current order status, with **Resume all**, **Hold all seated**, a selected front-truck release order, and forward-line resume. In a live owner-pilot Workbench preview, the panel was readable, mouse clicks on Hold/Resume reached completed server states, and **CLOSE MAP** returned to third-person vehicle view. Direct M-key opening and restored throttle/steering after closing remain unverified. The historical autonomous forward test called the server order directly and did not click the panel.

1. In the open World Editor, press `F5` to preview and enter as a US player at the base apron. The saved scene has three Convoy Driver groups and four trucks. Cargo loading remains manual.
2. To stage one or more drivers, use **Follow me on foot** while you are outside vehicles. The new build uses an entity FOLLOW waypoint per driver. When you enter a car, truck, or helicopter, each staged driver attempts a free cargo seat in that same vehicle, never the controls; on exit, they try to dismount and resume following on foot. If no seat is free or boarding times out after 25 seconds, they hold outside. **Stop following on foot** cancels a staged driver's order; only the player who gave it can issue the stop. Multiple walkers and passenger transport need a live test.
3. At the truck, use **Join my convoy in closest empty vehicle** on that staged driver, or use it immediately on an idle driver already beside a truck. The driver selects the nearest empty wheeled vehicle within the configured search radius, including a Jeep; check which one is closest. Wait until it takes the driver's seat. The first recruit becomes Unit One. Stay on foot to add more drivers, then enter your own vehicle to start moving.
4. While on foot, visit the second preplaced driver. Use **Join my convoy in closest empty vehicle** again, or use **Follow me on foot** to bring the driver to the desired vehicle first. Once it boards, Unit Two should track Unit One's truck rather than the player's vehicle. Recruit the third preplaced driver the same way to test Unit Three tracking Unit Two. Enter the remaining truck yourself after all orders are placed.
5. To test replacement, leave one preplaced driver idle or spawn another one-member Convoy Driver group and an empty wheeled vehicle. You may stage the candidate on foot first. **Replace convoy leader with this driver** boards the candidate before changing the convoy. Once the correct driver seat is confirmed, the old leader leaves the convoy and dismounts. The remaining units stay in order behind the new leader. If boarding fails, the old leader remains in charge.
6. **Stand down** removes a seated driver from this player's convoy; later units reconnect to the preceding unit. Stopping or getting out of the player's vehicle should bring the trucks closer while the drivers stay seated. Reentering and driving away should resume ordinary following if no unloading maneuver is underway.
7. Observe the actual order and spacing of the vehicles, which vehicle each NPC selected, whether anyone stops or gets stuck, and which spoken calls are heard. The leader should warn at 180 m sustained separation from the player, then become lost after 10 seconds beyond 275 m. Later units use those thresholds relative to their immediate predecessor. Check `[ConvoyFollower]` log entries alongside what happened on screen; the log alone does not prove movement or audio playback.

For a supply handoff test, add two more one-member driver groups and two M923A1 transport trucks to the prepared scene, then order all five drivers. Drive far enough to establish an approach route, stop in a clear area, and inspect the rear cargo menu of the front assigned truck. Load and unload supplies yourself. For a clear, wide road, also try the panel's **Pull ahead and wait** alternative with the lead parked off the lane. Watch the actual released truck path, parked position, and next truck's arrival at the same bay. A rear return line should be reactivated by driving homeward past it; a forward waiting line requires driving past the farthest truck and explicitly resuming it. If an order fails or the road is blocked, record positions and `[ConvoyFollower]` logs; a waypoint or accepted command alone does not prove a safe maneuver. The rear order is installed on M923A1 and standard Ural4320 transport cargo parts; the latter still needs a live menu and road smoke test. Covered Ural inheritance and other vehicles remain outside the verified interaction scope.

When a predecessor stops for three seconds, a follower more than 14 m away gets one final road-navigated waypoint update with a 10 m center completion radius. This aims to close the line without sending a truck to an off-road point or repeatedly restarting its route. After three 30-second stall checks with less than 3 m movement while more than 40 m behind, or when the assigned vehicle reaches the explicit `DESTROYED` state, the driver stands down and attempts to dismount. ## Earlier combat and reboard investigation

In the polish preview, the log recorded one staged driver boarding a cargo seat, riding, and beginning to exit after the player exited. It also recorded `STOP_SETTLE` for all three convoy trucks, but their final visual spacing remains unverified. The player reported that all three drivers left their trucks as soon as hostile fire began; the log showed `ABORTED` for each, with no preceding mod-issued stand-down, vehicle-destroyed, or terminal-stall event. The game AI's exact internal trigger was not logged. The old under-fire monitor also logged that the driver damage manager was unavailable, so that preview could not verify the hit report.

The current build gives active convoy driving waypoints Game Master priority, above the documented autonomous get-out priority. An unexpected exit by a live driver with a viable assigned truck now preserves that unit's roster place while it tries the assigned driver seat up to three times, 12 seconds apart. Successful reboarding resumes the chain; a dead driver, destroyed truck, wrong seat, or exhausted attempts removes the unit. Explicit **Stand down** still removes it immediately. The damage monitor now retries late attachment; the reopened Workbench scene logged successful attachment to all three drivers. Later gameplay confirmed a hit-triggered leader call, but systematic driver-versus-vehicle and Soviet checks remain. At a stop or while the player is on foot, the move waypoint can finish or be cleared, so forced priority alone may not prevent an AI combat exit.

The session now ends after a short grace period if the ordering player disconnects or the original character is confirmed dead. It issues stand-down to every active and parked driver, then clears the old convoy so a new one can be ordered. Temporarily controlling another entity in Game Master pauses lead-vehicle detection without rebinding or ending the session. The code passed Workbench script validation, but still needs a live death/respawn check; a blocked get-out waypoint can leave a driver seated after the session ends.

For a repeat combat preview, press `F5`, order and board all three drivers, then check the convoy under hostile fire while moving and while stopped. Watch whether drivers stay seated or whether `REBOARD_STARTED`, `REBOARD_ATTEMPT`, `REBOARDED`, or `REBOARD_TERMINAL` appears in the log. If a truck or driver takes a confirmed hit, listen for the leader's under-fire call and check for `UNDER_FIRE_MONITOR: assigned vehicle damage manager attached` followed by `UNDER_FIRE`. Also visually check nose-to-tail spacing, the US crew clothing and M9, and whether more than one staged walker follows and rides as passengers. Near misses do not trigger the damage call.

## Live field note: vehicle choice

Under the earlier label **Drive nearest truck and follow me**, the driver took the player's Jeep. The script chooses the nearest empty wheeled vehicle within 35 m, and the user later saw that Jeep physically follow the lead vehicle. The current Start/Add actions say **closest empty vehicle** so that scope is clear; an unintended assignment is still possible when multiple vehicles are nearby. The user is continuing with nearest-vehicle selection for now. A later UX pass should let the player designate the target vehicle, for example by interacting with it or aiming at/selecting it, and visibly identify or confirm the target before the order is issued. Keep that interaction simple.

There is no driver-facing voice toggle. The packaged `Configs/CF_ConvoySettings.conf` keeps the player's recorded voice at `m_iVoicePack 0`; pack 1 adds generated alternate phrasings. The same settings control routine call chance, routine spacing, repeated routine phrases, and repeated range/rejoined status. Stuck, lost, and under-fire reports are not dropped by the server debounce; incoming exceptions remove stale queued routine chatter before playback. ## Asset structure

Game Master has US and Soviet **Convoy Driver** groups. The US group contains a crew AI character with an M9; the Soviet group uses Soviet crew gear and a PM. Spawn a group card, not a character prefab directly: the behavior uses the driver's parent `SCR_AIGroup` to assign boarding and follow waypoints. The Soviet card and interaction have passed Workbench validation and packing, but still need a live smoke test.

The character prefab inherits the game's `Character_US_Crew.et`, including its replicated character setup, and adds `CF_DriverControllerComponent` and `CF_UnderFireMonitorComponent`. It clears the crew carbine slot, equips an M9 in the secondary slot, and replaces the rifle magazines with three M9 magazines. It appends `CF_FollowOnFootAction`, `CF_StopOnFootAction`, `CF_AssignTruckAction` (Join), `CF_ReplaceLeaderAction`, and `CF_StandDownAction` to the inherited `ActionsManagerComponent` under the existing `default` interaction context. The component IDs were inspected from the installed base game's `data007.pak` (version 1.8.0.13), not guessed.

The Soviet character inherits `Character_USSR_Crew.et`, clears its inherited primary weapon, equips a PM and three magazines, and adds the same convoy controller, damage monitor, and interactions. Its one-member Soviet group is registered in the same placeable group catalog as the US group.

The unload order is a separate additive override of the vanilla `M923A1_cargo.et` part. It appends `CF_ReleaseAtUnloadAction` to that part's existing `door_rear` action context, alongside vanilla supply load/unload and cargo storage actions. The M923A1 transport slot registers the part's actions with the truck. Its visibility is owner-checked and limited to the seated front convoy driver after arrival; no truck door or extra handle is required. The matching prefab override passed a headless pack check, but the rear scroll menu still needs a player-facing check.

The same action is appended to the vanilla Ural4320 cargo part's existing `door_rear` context. The Soviet prefab and Ural override were checked against the installed base-game pack, then validated and packed headlessly. The Ural rear menu still needs a player-facing check.

The group is added to Game Master through an explicit placeable group registry referenced from the additive `EditorModeEdit.et` override. This follows Bohemia's sample registration path, but still needs an in-game spawn check; the prepared scene's preplaced driver does not verify the card. The standalone character is deliberately absent from the registry, because direct placement may leave it without a parent AI group.

Primary source patterns: Bohemia's `SampleMod_NewFaction` group prefab, placeable registry and editor override; Bohemia's Game Master editable entities documentation; the installed game's `Character_Base.et` component IDs and action context. No vanilla files were changed.
