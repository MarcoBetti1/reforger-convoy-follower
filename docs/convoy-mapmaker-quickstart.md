# Convoy Follower: map-maker quickstart

Convoy Follower adds US and Soviet **Convoy Driver** AI groups that a player can order into empty vehicles. The first driver follows the player's vehicle; later drivers follow the vehicle immediately ahead. Players load and unload supplies themselves. The current build also includes a rear-menu order on M923A1 transports and standard-body Ural4320 transports for moving an unloaded truck out of the stopping spot.

## Add it to a scenario

1. Add **Convoy Follower** as a dependency of your scenario project in Workbench's Resource Manager project options. Make sure the dependency is available in the Workbench launcher when opening the scenario. See Bohemia's [mod project setup](https://community.bistudio.com/wiki/Arma_Reforger%3AMod_Project_Setup) and [project dependency options](https://community.bistudio.com/wiki/Arma_Reforger%3AResource_Manager%3A_Options).
2. In World Editor, place `Prefabs/Groups/Group_CF_Driver.et` for a US driver or `Prefabs/Groups/Group_CF_Driver_USSR.et` for a Soviet driver, once per convoy truck. Place the **group**, not a character prefab directly: each driver must have its own one-member AI group for waypoint orders. Both groups have Game Master registrations. The Soviet group and interaction still need a live in-game check.
3. Place an empty, drivable wheeled vehicle near each driver. Use vanilla **M923A1 transport** or standard-body **Ural4320 transport** trucks for the unloading sequence: the addon adds **Pull off and regroup** and **Pull ahead and wait** to their rear cargo parts. Live scroll-menu visibility on both vehicle types still needs checking. The current nearest-vehicle interaction searches a short radius and can choose a Jeep if it is closer than the intended truck. Leave a separate vehicle for the player. Add a player spawn and any supplies or destinations your scenario needs.
4. Load the scenario with Convoy Follower enabled for the host/server and players. The addon project declares Arma Reforger as its base dependency; your scenario should declare Convoy Follower as its dependency.

The prepared example is `Worlds/Tests/ConvoyFollower_Arland.ent`: it places a US spawn, three driver groups, and four empty M923A1 transport trucks. Add two more one-member driver groups and two assigned trucks to test a five-truck unloading line. Open it with:

```powershell
npm run workbench:open -- --editor world --project addons/ConvoyFollower/addon.gproj --world Worlds/Tests/ConvoyFollower_Arland.ent --authorize-local-test-scripts --execute
```

Press **F5** in World Editor to preview this local test. The opt-in authorization flag applies only to this Workbench launch and avoids the script authorization dialog during automated F5 tests.

For a longer staged run, [the Everon night demo](convoy-everon-demo.md) has a southern US spawn, an LAV for the player, three truck/driver pairs, a supply source, and an unload location near Levie. Its scenario header requests 22:00; bare F5 preview may inherit the parent world's time.

For Soviet parity, open `Worlds/Tests/ConvoyFollower_Arland_USSR.ent` in the same addon. It places an USSR spawn, three Soviet driver groups beside three Ural4320 transports, and a UAZ for the player. Use **Join my convoy** on each driver, then test the front Ural's rear **Pull off and regroup** action at a stopped arrival. This scene passed headless validation and packaging; Soviet player controls and the Ural rear menu still need an in-game check. Its staging apron is reused from the US Game Master test and is not the vanilla Soviet Conflict base.

## What players do

### Destination and return in four steps

1. **Stop and pull up.** Park the player's lead vehicle on a clear road and let the trucks settle behind it. The owner may stay seated while they arrive; stopping or getting out does not release a truck.
2. **Unload and release the front truck.** Get out, unload its supplies yourself, then stand within 2.5 m of the rear cargo area of the front occupied M923A1 or standard Ural4320 transport. The player who started the convoy can choose **Pull off and regroup** in the existing rear scroll menu once that truck has reached the stopping spot. This order sends only that truck toward a nearby return slot.
3. **Wait for the next truck.** The next truck approaches the same unloading spot only after the released truck physically clears the bay and parks. Repeat the unload and rear-menu order for each truck you want to release. If the release order is unavailable because there is no safe road slot, drive to a clearer place. If an accepted release fails and the outbound line holds, use **Resume convoy following** at the front truck's rear menu before driving on.
4. **Choose the next direction.** To return, drive the player's lead vehicle homeward past the parked return line; the intended behavior is to merge parked and still-waiting trucks into one following convoy. **Regroup convoy for return** at a parked truck's rear menu is the explicit alternative. Driving onward with no return trigger resumes only the unreleased trucks; parked trucks keep waiting. The return crossing and road maneuvers still need live gameplay verification.

### Alternative at an open roadside: pull ahead and wait

**Pull ahead and wait** is available for the front truck from its M923A1 or standard Ural4320 rear cargo scroll menu, or from that truck's selected row in the convoy map panel. Park your lead vehicle on a clear shoulder at least about 8 m off the truck's driving lane, leaving a connected road at least 8 m wide ahead and space for the front truck to pass; then wait for it to settle at the stop. The server checks mapped road width, a connected unoccupied road slot, and whether the lead blocks the driving lane. If any preflight fails, the order is rejected with a reason; an accepted click alone does not mean the truck has parked. On the surveyed width-8 test road, an autonomous run physically parked two trucks ahead and brought the third into the unload bay. The test lead could not pass the parked line, so physical owner passage and rejoining are still unverified. The player-facing panel's Hold and Resume commands have been clicked in a live preview; this forward order and its rear-menu entry have not yet been clicked live.

Trucks parked in the forward waiting line do not automatically rejoin when the player starts driving. Drive the lead vehicle past the farthest forward truck, then use the panel's **Resume ahead line after passing** command to rejoin them behind you. The server rejects the command until the player is driving past that line. This return interaction also needs a live player test.

- Approach an idle Convoy Driver on foot. Use **Follow me on foot** to bring a driver toward a vehicle, or give a convoy order while the driver is already beside an empty vehicle.
- Use **Join my convoy in closest empty vehicle** on each driver while on foot. The first recruit becomes Unit One; each later recruit joins behind the current tail. Wait for the driver seats to fill, then drive away in the player's vehicle.
- The first convoy truck follows the player; each later truck follows the truck ahead. The leader's prerecorded radio calls report convoy status to the ordering player. Loading and unloading supplies remain manual.
- **Replace convoy leader with this driver** boards a new leader first, then releases the old one. **Stand down** removes a driver. When the player's vehicle stops for several seconds, the trucks close toward it and stay seated. Getting out also starts this arrival behavior. Driving away resumes ordinary following when no unloading maneuver is in progress.
- At an unloading stop, unload the front M923A1 or Ural4320 cargo truck manually. From outside at its rear cargo menu, the player who ordered the convoy can choose **Pull off and regroup**. This is an explicit order for that truck to move to a reachable road slot behind the remaining line. The truck first tries a clear staged turn; when the road has no lateral turn stage, it sends a direct road waypoint and lets the vehicle AI plan the turn. The next truck advances only after the released truck reaches and settles clear of the bay. Repeat for each truck being unloaded; simply stopping or exiting never releases a truck. If the route fails and the outbound line holds, use the same rear menu's **Resume convoy following** command to cancel that stop and drive to a better place. Ural menu visibility and driving behavior still need a live check.
- The parked return trucks wait instead of following the player automatically. If the player drives homeward past the parked return line, the intended return event merges them with any unreleased trucks; those unreleased trucks turn once before following the player's vehicle. As an explicit alternative, the owner can use **Regroup convoy for return** at the rear of any parked return truck, then drive away in the lead vehicle. If the player instead continues outbound, the unreleased trucks resume following while the parked return line waits. **Resume convoy following** lets the owner continue the unreleased line after a failed unload attempt; it does not activate the parked return line. The manual return command waits for the trucks to park and can decline if an unload maneuver is still in progress.
- Staged on-foot drivers try available passenger seats when the player enters a vehicle and try to resume walking after the player exits. **Stop following on foot** cancels a staging order.
- Voice calls are controlled by the addon configuration, not by a player action on a driver. Routine calls are randomized and spaced apart; the first stuck, lost, and under-fire reports remain unconditional.

## Tune the addon in Workbench

Addon authors can edit `Configs/CF_ConvoySettings.conf` in the Workbench Config Editor and rebuild. Dedicated server owners can instead copy [this example JSON](convoy-server-settings.example.json) to `<server -profile directory>/profile/ConvoyFollowerSettings.json`, edit it, and restart the server. The addon reads this optional `$profile:` file once on the authoritative server; omitted keys keep packaged defaults. For instance, `"m_bVoiceEnabled": false` mutes all private convoy calls, while `"m_iVoicePack": 1` selects the [optional generated leader](convoy-generated-voice.md). Voice pack 0 retains the player's original recordings. The same profile override accepts every field in `CF_ConvoySettings.c`, including moving/stopped spacing, the hard five-vehicle cap, range thresholds, stall/reboard timing, and routine call chance and cooldowns. Invalid numeric ranges are clamped. This does **not** create an in-game menu or use arbitrary keys in Reforger's main server-config JSON. Return turn distance and slot geometry are not yet exposed here. The AI driving order priority and one-driver-per-AI-group rule remain fixed because the controller depends on them.

## Boundaries and test status

- Keep each driver in a separate one-member AI group. Multiple soldiers inside one group are not supported by the current waypoint controller.
- During ordinary outbound driving, a truck that passes the vehicle it follows holds instead of chasing a waypoint behind itself. The server can swap two adjacent drivers in the chain after their same-road order remains clear for several seconds; it leaves ambiguous bends and unloading/return maneuvers untouched. Test this with two and three trucks before relying on recovery in a public scenario.
- This addon does not choose delivery destinations, load cargo, or unload cargo. The player drives the lead vehicle and gives the driver orders. No map-maker return marker is required by the current code; it derives nearby road positions from the route and vehicles. Leave a clear road and room for the convoy to turn and park. A blocked or narrow road can prevent the return maneuver.
- The **Pull off and regroup**, **Pull ahead and wait**, **Resume convoy following**, and **Regroup convoy for return** rear-menu integrations are scoped to the vanilla M923A1 transport cargo part and the standard Ural4320 transport cargo part. The new forward rear-menu option is script-validated but its visibility in the live scroll menu is unverified. Covered Ural inheritance and other wheeled vehicles need separate checks; they may be selected by the nearest-vehicle boarding action but should not be assumed to have these rear actions. A narrow, blocked, or disconnected road can still prevent parking. The driver remains in the convoy if release fails, and preflight failure does not play a false stuck call.
- A prototype convoy panel opens over the normal map when the owner has an active convoy. It shows server roster/state rows and offers **Resume all**, **Hold all seated**, selected front-truck release orders, and **Resume ahead line after passing**. The normal map supplies cursor handling. In a live owner-pilot F5 test, the panel was legible, mouse clicks on Hold/Resume reached completed server states, and **CLOSE MAP** returned to third-person vehicle view. Direct M-key opening and restored throttle/steering after closing remain unverified. Nearby driver interactions and existing rear cargo actions remain available.
- The group is registered for Game Master through `Configs/Editor/PlaceableEntities/CF_Groups.conf` and the additive `EditorModeEdit.et` override. A scenario can place the group prefab directly even when it does not use Game Master.
- Three-truck following and leader voice playback were observed in a live preview. A later live test logged passenger riding, confirmed-hit radio playback, leader loss and chain retargeting. The new stopped pull-up and unloading/return code passed Workbench script validation and packaging on September 24, 2026, but still needs an in-game check of the driving paths, rear-menu visibility, five-truck handoff, and return crossing. Sustained driving after a middle truck is lost, exact stopped spacing, and public multiplayer use also need direct player checks.

For addon authors, [Bohemia's Game Master editable entity guide](https://community.bistudio.com/wiki/Arma_Reforger%3AGame_Master%3A_Editable_Entities_Configuration) explains the group registration pattern. For publishing a separate scenario that depends on this addon, see the [mod publishing process](https://community.bistudio.com/wiki/Arma_Reforger%3AMod_Publishing_Process).
