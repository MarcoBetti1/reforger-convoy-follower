# Vehicle Gap Shortlist

Date of research: `2026-04-02`

This list is not a mathematically perfect proof of absence. It is a practical gap analysis based on current Arma Reforger workshop searches and local installed addon inspection.

I excluded vehicles that already show obvious dedicated workshop entries or dependency chains, including:

- Toyota / Hilux technicals
- Gatoma pickup variants
- Land Rover Series III
- Cold War M880
- UAZ-452 / UAZ-469 civ variants

## Best Candidate: Volkswagen / Bombardier Iltis

Why it stands out:

- Cold War correct.
- Distinct NATO vehicle that is not yet overrepresented.
- Small, simple, and much more achievable than a tracked or articulated vehicle.
- Historically flexible.

Historical basis:

- Volkswagen states the `Iltis` was built for military service from the late 1970s into the 1980s and used by Germany and Belgium.
- The Canadian Iltis documentation shows it replaced older jeep fleets and had multiple fielded variants, including a `TOW` launcher version, command/recon, ambulance, and cargo-tailgate versions.

Why it fits Reforger:

- Reforger already supports jeep/light-truck style driving, open soft-top layouts, pintle/mount patterns, and small utility vehicles.
- A base Iltis can become a whole family:
  - utility
  - command/recon
  - ambulance
  - MG variant
  - future ATGM variant

Recommended initial variants:

- `Iltis Utility`
- `Iltis Command`
- `Iltis MG`

## Strong Second Candidate: Steyr-Puch Pinzgauer 710M

Why it stands out:

- Cold War accurate.
- Very recognizable silhouette.
- Historically used by Austria, Switzerland, the UK, Saudi Arabia, and others.
- Not obviously present in current workshop search results.

Historical basis:

- The first-generation `710/712` Pinzgauer entered production in `1971`.
- It is a high-mobility military utility vehicle designed for mobility and general utility, not as an armored combat vehicle.
- The `710M` could carry large crews or cargo loads.

Why it fits Reforger:

- Excellent candidate for:
  - utility truck
  - radio truck
  - supply truck
  - MG truck
- It gives you something NATO-adjacent without feeling like another HMMWV clone.

Cost/risk:

- More work than an Iltis because of its larger, more unusual body.
- Still far easier than a tracked APC.

## Third Candidate: Volvo C303 / Tgb 11

Why it stands out:

- Cold War Scandinavian military truck, very distinctive, almost no workshop competition found.
- Highly mobile and visually memorable because of portal axles and high ground clearance.

Historical basis:

- Developed in the late `1960s`, produced from `1974`.
- Used by Sweden and exported for military and utility roles.

Why it fits Reforger:

- Great for:
  - utility variant
  - radio/signal variant
  - winter/UN livery variant
  - MG variant

Cost/risk:

- Moderate modeling effort.
- Less obvious to casual players than a Hilux or jeep, but stronger as a unique mod identity.

## Fourth Candidate: M274 Mechanical Mule

Why it stands out:

- Extremely unique.
- Strong Cold War U.S. identity.
- No obvious dedicated workshop hit found.

Historical basis:

- The M274 was introduced in `1956` and used until the `1980s`.
- It was a lightweight cargo platform and also served as a mount for weapons including the `106mm recoilless rifle`.

Why it fits Reforger:

- Great for:
  - airborne logistics
  - ammo carrier
  - recoilless rifle carrier
  - field MG carrier

Cost/risk:

- Smaller art scope than most vehicles.
- Gameplay niche is narrower than a normal truck.
- Best if we want a standout “why has nobody done this yet?” mod.

## Fifth Candidate: M561 Gama Goat

Why it stands out:

- Very Cold War U.S.
- Very different from the usual trucks and pickups.
- No obvious dedicated workshop hit found.

Historical basis:

- In service in the `1970s–1990s`.
- Semi-amphibious, articulated `6x6`, used heavily in Vietnam-era logistics and mobility roles.

Why it fits Reforger:

- Could be outstanding for:
  - supply
  - transport
  - ambulance
  - command variant

Cost/risk:

- Highest technical risk on this list because the articulated chassis is the whole point of the vehicle.
- This is not the first vehicle I would pick for a clean modding workflow.

## Recommendation

If the goal is:

- `Best balance of originality, authenticity, and feasibility`: build the `Iltis`.
- `Best visually unique NATO utility truck`: build the `Pinzgauer 710M`.
- `Best niche U.S. Cold War idea`: build the `M274 Mule`.
- `Most ambitious engineering challenge`: build the `Gama Goat`.

## My Recommendation

Build the `Volkswagen / Bombardier Iltis` first.

Reason:

- It is historically accurate.
- It fills a real workshop gap.
- It can scale into a family of variants.
- It is much more likely to become a fully playable mod in a reasonable amount of time than the Gama Goat or a new armored car.
